Featured Project

Early Warning Student Success Dashboard

The Early Warning Student Success Dashboard is an education-focused web application designed to help educators identify students who may benefit from additional support or intervention.

The project combines data-driven decision making, web development, system design, testing, and educational problem-solving into a functional prototype.
